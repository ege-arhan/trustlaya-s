counts describe source releases; actual evaluated counts will be recorded

**Gold-label rule:** no LLM-generated labels are treated as gold. TAB's PII
task is separate from document-level TrustLaya `pii` classification and its
pattern-assisted evidence spans. TrustAIRLab's author-provided jailbreak
label is evaluated as jailbreak, not silently relabeled as tool-output
injection. No unavailable clinical or secret corpus will be scraped or
represented by fabricated scores.
