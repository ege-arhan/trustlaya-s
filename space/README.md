# TrustLaya-S · Safety Triage

A research demo for Turkish-first AI safety triage. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/xzwq/TrustLaya-S) · [Code and measured results](https://github.com/ege-arhan/trustlaya-s).

Model scores are temperature-scaled on synthetic validation data only. They do not represent validated real-world risk probabilities. An independent policy engine sets the final action. Please do not enter real personal data or credentials into this public demo.
